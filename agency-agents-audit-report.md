## Cross-Cutting Observations

### Strengths in Repository
1. **Deep specialist coverage** in engineering, finance, marketing — multiple qualified candidates per division
2. **Methodological rigor** — agents use frameworks (DMAIC, PRISMA, ADR, VSM, SIPOC)
3. **Production orientation** — emphasis on observability, failure modes, SLAs, audit readiness
4. **Structured deliverables** — every agent includes templates, workflows, success metrics

### Gaps Requiring New Agent Creation
| Division | Missing Roles | Priority |
|----------|--------------|----------|
| Developer | Engineering Manager, Security Architect, Data Engineer | High |
| Marketing | Product Marketing, Events/Field Marketing, Analytics/Attribution | Medium |
| Finance | Treasury/Cash Management, FP&A Manager (senior), Investor Relations | Medium |
| Creative | Creative Director, Design Systems Lead, UX Research Lead, Copywriter | High |
| Research & Intelligence | Competitive Intelligence, Market Researcher, Data Analyst, OSINT Specialist | High |
| Business Operations | People Operations, Legal/Compliance, Facilities/Workspace, Procurement | Medium |

### Agents Not Evaluated (Out of Scope)
- Healthcare, hospitality, real estate, legal, government, education specialists
- Language translators, personal growth, study abroad
- Sales, customer service, retail, supply chain
- Niche engineering (embedded, electrical, IoT, blockchain, mobile)
