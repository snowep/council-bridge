## Research Methodology

### Evidence Classification Standard (4-Tier)

| Classification | Definition | Standard |
|----------------|------------|----------|
| **VERIFIED** | Directly supported by reliable public evidence | Primary sources: recorded interviews, documentaries, speeches, published writings, court records, official statements |
| **STRONGLY INFERRED** | Supported by multiple consistent observations across independent sources | 3+ independent sources showing consistent patterns |
| **WEAKLY INFERRED** | Plausible but insufficiently documented | 1-2 sources, or patterns with some conflicting evidence |
| **UNKNOWN** | No reliable evidence available | No public evidence found |

### Source Priority Hierarchy
1. **Primary Sources** — Recorded interviews, documentaries, public speeches, published writings by the subject
2. **Authorized Biographies** — Works with subject cooperation/access
3. **Reputable Profiles** — Major publications (NYT, Vogue, WSJ, Bloomberg, Rolling Stone, etc.)
4. **Documented Professional Decisions** — Court filings, SEC documents, official company communications
5. **Social Media / Public Statements** — Verified accounts, official channels

### What Was Explicitly Avoided
- Secondhand descriptions without primary source verification
- Fan theories, speculative forums, unverified social media
- Reduction to famous quotations only
- Invention of private thoughts, experiences, or relationships
- Artificial consistency where public evidence shows contradiction
