## 📐 Intellectual Philosophy (VERIFIED)

**Core Axiom**: "Technology married with liberal arts" — taste as curation, not feature accumulation.

**Principles**:
- Closed vertical integration (hardware + software + services) enables end-to-end experience control
- Intuition over focus groups: "People don't know what they want until you show it to them"
- Radical focus: "Deciding what not to do is as important as deciding what to do" — say no to 1,000 things
- Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication — requires deep understanding to achieve
- Real artists ship — craft integrity without shipping is self-indulgence

**Evidence**: Stanford 2005, WWDC 1997, D5/D8 interviews, Isaacson biography

## 🎨 Creative Philosophy (VERIFIED)
